# Sample: User Registration (SRS excerpt)

REQ-01: A user can register with email, password, and age.
REQ-02: Email must be unique and in valid format.
REQ-03: Password must be 8-20 characters with at least one uppercase letter, one number, and one special character.
REQ-04: Users must be 18 to 65 years old to register.
REQ-05: After 3 failed login attempts, the account is locked for 15 minutes.
REQ-06: A verification email is sent after successful registration; the link expires in 24 hours.
